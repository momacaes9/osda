OSDA AI — One Step Drives All

OSDA (OSDA AI · OSDA Global · Operação Sem Danos Ambientais) is an international environmental engineering project led by young Brazilian women 🇧🇷. Make your lifestyle sustainable with AI: a free sustainability questionnaire, a CO2 footprint calculator and an AI chatbot, in English and Portuguese.
